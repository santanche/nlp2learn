A Tiny Neural Network: Two Softplus Paths

Seeds germinate poorly in cold soil, well in warm soil, and die when the soil gets too hot. No single S-shaped curve fits this "only the middle works" pattern, but a network with one input, two hidden softplus neurons and one output can: each path bends a softplus curve, and their weighted sum produces the bump. Pick a sample to follow its computation through the network step by step.
