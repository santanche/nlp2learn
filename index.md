Examples, laboratories, and exercises for the **Natural Language Processing**
discipline. This site collects the entry points to the material used in
class.

## Get started

- 🚀 **[Run this repository on Binder](https://mybinder.org/v2/gh/santanche/nlp2learn/main)**
  — launch the notebooks in a ready-to-use environment, no local setup
  required (configuration in [`binder/`](https://github.com/santanche/nlp2learn/tree/main/binder)).

## Laboratories & projects

- 📚 **[Case Teaching data](https://github.com/santanche/nlp2learn/tree/main/data/case-teaching-cabot)**
  — historical case-teaching corpus (Cabot), extracted text and metadata
  used for information extraction exercises.
- 🧠 **[From Clinical Case Reports to Knowledge Graphs](https://github.com/santanche/nlp2learn/tree/main/to-kg)**
  — a full pedagogical activity taking clinical case reports through
  tokenization and entity/relation extraction into a knowledge graph.
- 🗂️ **[Projects 2026](https://github.com/santanche/nlp2learn/tree/main/projects/2026)**
  — the project track and templates for the 2026 class.
- 🧟 **[ZombieGPT]({{ "/language-model/zombiegpt/zombiegpt.html" | relative_url }})**
  — an interactive, minimal probabilistic language model to explore n-gram
  language modeling concepts.
